# Reflection and Note

## 19 Oct 2026 in Gawan 1-2 year old Toddler Room

S: Toddler children were doing free outdoor play, but they had too much energy. Some cry easily because of unexpected pushing or rough behaviour.

T: The task for me was to settle the children instead of being dominated by emotional and downstairs brain.

A: I started my intervention by singing "Freeze Dance" music to initiate the physical motion. The children started to follow my rhyme and dance, such as skipping and turning around, etc. I knew that one simple activity cannot engage them for long. So when it ended, I turned to a similar but more natural movement song, "Walking in the Jungle". The song is nice as we can walk, jump, and skip with the instructions and identify different animal sounds. When we stop and listen, the children also practiced mood regulation, listening ears, and being respectful to wait. It was then followed by a yoga activity. For those mini children, we make the idea easy, such as being "Stars (stretching arms and legs)", "Mountain (Standing still and closing our palm up on our heads)", "Hourse (Stretching crus 工字步)", "rainbow (stretching two sides of our arms and waist)", "reaching high", "Elephant (stretching bellies)". Finally, the children sat down in the imaginary bush around me. I started to make up some stories about my friends "Freddie, Billy and ...animals"

R: The children were engaged, settled, and were emotionally regulated step by step from pure movement (Freeze dance), to switching between loud and quiet movement (walking in the jungle), and then quiet movement (Yoga) and quietly listening (storytelling).
